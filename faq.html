Before You Buy, Know This.

The important details about authenticity, rights, delivery, and using reactions in your campaigns.

Are the reactions from real people? +

Yes. Every reaction comes from a real creator, not an AI-generated face.

Do I get commercial usage rights? +

Yes. Each reaction clearly states the included usage rights before you purchase.

How quickly will I receive my content? +

Most available reactions can be licensed instantly, while custom requests can be delivered within 24–48 hours.

Can I use reactions in paid ads? +

Yes, as long as your selected license includes paid advertising usage.

What happens if a reaction doesn’t fit my campaign? +

You can preview the content before purchasing, so you only pay for reactions you actually want.
